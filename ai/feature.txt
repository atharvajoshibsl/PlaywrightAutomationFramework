AK-101 - Product listing page: shell, controls and product cards

Type:      Story
Priority:  Highest
Component: Catalogue
Status:    Ready for QA


Summary
-------
A visitor landing on the shop root sees the whole product catalogue together
with the site shell, so they can orient themselves and start browsing without
signing in.


Background
----------
The shop seeds 16 products across 4 categories, with 53 variants between them.
Every visitor gets their own copy of that data, so a first-time listing looks
identical for everyone.


Acceptance criteria
-------------------

Page
- The root URL serves the product listing.
- The browser tab reads "Products | AItomationKart".
- The page heading reads "Products".

Header, present on every page
- The wordmark reads "AItomationKart".
- A tagline reads "You read it wrong - read it correct through automation!".
  It sits beside the wordmark, and is hidden on windows narrower than 1180px so
  that it cannot collide with the search box.
- A search box with a Search button beside it.
- A theme toggle.
- A Cart link carrying a count badge. For a visitor with an empty cart the badge
  reads 0.
- A guest sees "Sign in" and "Register". The signed-in header is AK-140.

Filter and sort controls
- Category chips, in this order: All, Apparel, Electronics, Footwear,
  Home & Kitchen. "All" is the active chip when the page first loads.
- A "Sort by" dropdown offering, in this order: Relevance, Price: low to high,
  Price: high to low, Name: A to Z, Rating. Relevance is the default.

Listing
- A result count reading "16 products found" when nothing is filtered.
- One card per product, so 16 cards on a first load.
- Every card carries an image, the brand, the product name, the price, a star
  rating, and a stock badge reading either "In stock" or "Out of stock".
- Prices are shown in rupees, with the currency symbol.
- Clicking a card opens that product's detail page.

Footer, present on every page
- The wordmark and the tagline are repeated.
- A list of the five frameworks the site exists to be automated with:
  Playwright, Selenium, Cypress, Robot Framework, REST API.
- A note stating that no real payments are taken.
- A copyright line naming Atharva Joshi.


Out of scope
------------
- Search, category filtering and sorting behaviour. That is AK-102.
- The product detail page. That is AK-103.
- Layouts below tablet width.
